SIDE A / THE QUARTET

Old-school sound.
Our kind of spin.

There's something about a great record. The warmth. The character. The way it brings people together.

That's the spirit behind LP Select. We're four guys from Albuquerque bringing our own energy to the timeless tradition of barbershop: four voices, close chords, and a sound that's bigger than the sum of its parts.

No backing tracks. No instruments. Just the joy of making harmony together.
